## What was clear

- **Download and Copy.** All eight buttons worked. Each message says what happened and what to do next, and stays on screen: "The text of “mockup.html” was copied. Paste it into a file of that name." The copied text equals the downloaded file. With copying refused: "Copying is blocked here. Use the download button."
- **The downloaded mockup looks like the preview.** I compared `mockup.html` with the Studio's preview for Soft Daylight with a changed menu, the same with square corners, and Midnight Aurora. They match, and the mockup does not scroll sideways at 375.
- **A changed Decision shows in the file where I can find it.** After I changed Corners and Motion, the heading read "(3 of 23 Steps decided)", 2.17 read "picked “Square corners”" with a "Rejected" line, and `tokens.css` changed.
- **PRESCRIPTION.md is well ordered.** "How to read this file", "Decision: none. The value of the start stands", "Add none" for Overlays, and "This file makes no claim about any standard. It states what was measured." are plain and honest. Section 6 matches the screen's contrast count (Poster Block: 3), and "Fix for me" reports "adjusted, and measured again at 4.55 to 1".
- **An odd project name does no harm.** A name with code fences and a fake heading stayed inside the "quoted data" block.
- **Fine details.** The intro says clearly that I do not choose them. Each opened text explains the detail with an everyday picture, such as the tails of g, p, and y. The second sentence follows the design: dark Directions say "The page tells the browser that it is dark." "Written on 2026-11-15; no person has reviewed it yet." is honest.
- **A Direction of my own: the form.** Empty name or spaces only: "Give the Direction a name." A name in use, in any letter case or matching a built-in name: "Another Direction already has the name “…”. Choose another name." The Studio writes the mood sentence when I leave it empty. `<b>` and `<img …>` show as plain text.
- **Start, remove, and file.** "Start again from “Harbor calm”?" and "Remove “Second look” for good?" say exactly what will be lost. File messages are exact: "Added 1 Direction of your own. 1 was left out: this browser already holds it." A project file opened as own Directions, and the reverse, are both refused in words.
- **375 pixels wide.** The Directions screen, a Step (pick button fixed at the bottom), the Summary, the rename form, and the remove question all fit. The one exception is U6-20.
