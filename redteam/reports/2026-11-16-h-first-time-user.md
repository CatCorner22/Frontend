## What was clear and good

- The first screen says what the tool is for: choose a look in small Steps, end with a project file and seven files for an AI coding tool, and "the line under the top bar says whether your work is saved".
- Changing a Direction after I had made decisions asked me first. The dialog said how many Decisions would be cleared, that Undo can bring them back, and that a copy is kept. "Keep my project" worked.
- Opening a project file asked me to confirm. Bad files (.json without the right content, and .txt) were refused with a clear message, "Your open project is unchanged."
- Text limits tell me: "This field is full: it holds 60 characters, and anything beyond that was left out." An emptied required field says "The page needs this text. The last one stays in place until you type a new one."
- Odd characters (tags, quotes, emoji, right-to-left marks, backticks) were shown as plain text. The exported mockup had no script tag and made no network requests. PRESCRIPTION.md fenced my answer safely, using four backticks when my text contained three.
- Every download gave a plain message ("handed to your browser to save, usually into your Downloads folder"). Copy gave a clear failure ("Copying is blocked here. Use the download button.").
- Reload mid-way kept my Directions, Decisions and place in the Steps. The browser Back and Forward buttons worked, with one address per Step.
- No page-wide sideways scroll at 1440, 1024, 768 or 390. The header, Step list and Summary reflowed without overlap. I found no low-contrast text in the Studio itself.
- The page names what was and was not checked (PRESCRIPTION.md section 6, section 7). It makes no "accessible" claims.

## What I did not try

- Own Directions: saving one, the "Your own Directions as a file" download and its reopening.
- The Overlay checkboxes (ticking, and seeing the effect in the preview and files).
- "Fix for me" and "Off" for Contrast checks beyond reading the labels. I only selected "Fix for me" once and did not look at its changes.
- Every Skin chip, and "Dress the Studio itself in this design" (not tried).
- The Phone and Tablet toggles in each preview, except default views.
- "Start a new project…" (clicked in a run that stopped early; not checked) and "Download this kept copy" / "Remove this kept copy".
- Keyboard-only use, a screen reader, zoomed text, and a dark-mode browser setting.
- All 28 Directions and all 23 Steps in detail. I walked through the 23 Steps once with defaults and read most of the text.
- Opening the downloaded zip on a real double-click, and building a site from the files with an AI tool.
- tokens.json, theme.css and DESIGN.md contents beyond a quick look.
