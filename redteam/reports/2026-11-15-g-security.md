Two parsers (commonmark 0.31.2, markdown-it 14.1.0) found the same 44 headings and 2 code blocks every time, with no HTML node and no link. None changed the structure.
- `mockup.html` with every content field starting with `"><script>alert(1)</script><img src=https://evil.example/x onerror=alert(2)><style>@import url(https://evil.example/a.css);</style></title></style><base href=//evil.example/>`. Loaded in Chromium: 0 scripts, 0 images, 0 frames, links, or base elements, 1 style element, 0 `on…` attributes, 0 dialogs, 0 requests to an outside address. The page's own security policy is `default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; img-src data:; font-src data:; form-action 'none'; base-uri 'none'`.
- `tokens.css` and `tokens.json`: 1,500 random projects with every number at its lowest, its highest, or a hair above its lowest, in all three contrast settings. Every CSS line matched `--name: value;` with no `;`, `{`, `}`, `<`, or `\` inside the value; no `NaN`, `Infinity`, `undefined`, or `null`; the JSON parsed every time; nothing threw; the slowest bundle took 10 ms.
- Identifiers from a file: `direction` = `__proto__`, `constructor`, `toString`, `own-<script>`, `graphite-precision\n# H` all became the fixed words "a Direction that the Studio composed at random". Overlay identifiers `"><script>`, `__proto__`, `constructor` were left out with a note; a Skin `__proto__` was left out; Decisions filed under `__proto__`, `constructor`, `toString` were left out, and no object's prototype changed.
- The bundle downloaded from the running page (`t1.cjs`): `tokens.css`, `tokens.json`, and `mockup.html` held no outside address; the only `url(` values were `data:` and an in-page reference. The page made no outside request during any of my runs.
