## What I tried that failed

- **Reading local files:** A `file:///etc/passwd` iframe, `fetch`, XHR, and `window.open` all failed. The iframe stayed `about:blank`, and the page origin is `null`. No file content reached the listener.
- **Service workers:** Not available (`serviceWorker` is absent in the `about:blank` context).
- **File picker and top navigation:** No `showOpenFilePicker`. `location.href` to the listener was routed, not sent.
- **Breaking out of a code fence:** The receipt's `fenceFor` and the Studio's `quoted()` both held against runs of backticks, `~~~`, and fence-like text in the name, Brief answers, reason, and class names.
- **Markup and script through the Studio:** An XSS string (`<img onerror>`, `<script>`, template markers) in every content field, the Brief, the name, and a reason produced no script, dialog, error, or request in the Studio. The archive's `mockup.html` held the text escaped. `<script` appeared only inside fenced quoted blocks of PRESCRIPTION.md.
- **Project parameters:** Parameters are enums or number ranges (zod), so no CSS injection through them. The archive name comes from a hex fingerprint.
- **Archive writer:** `unzip -t` accepted the archive from a hostile project, with seven stored files.
- **Fingerprint checks on the input folder:** Symlinked `tokens.json` or `mockup.html` pointing at a secret gave only "fingerprint missing", with no content echoed.
- **Process cleanup:** No Chromium processes were left after hang, crash, or large-page runs.

## What I did not check

- Whether the pinned action hashes in `ci.yml` match the real releases, and whether the `@google/design.md` package is the genuine one (no network).
- A browser exploit or sandbox escape; Chromium's own bugs.
- The Fit check's behavior when Chromium makes its own background connections (a proxy log once showed a refused call to a Google host during a run; I could not tie it to my run).
- Full memory exhaustion of the host (one 16 GB allocation attempt only crashed the tab).
- The Studio's private-page runtime (`window.claude.*` downloads and storage), the research-rule and craft-rule texts, the e2e tests, and the `docs/knowledge` content.
- Whether an AI tool actually obeys hidden tag characters or the injected headings; I confirmed only that they reach the files.
