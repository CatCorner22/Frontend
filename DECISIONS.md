# Decisions

Each entry records a decision that shapes the project, or a departure from `PLAN.md`, with the reason and who approved it.

**How to read the last column.**
- *Owner* means that the owner stated the decision or the approval in words. `docs/owner-instructions.md` quotes those words.
- *Builder's design under the owner's instruction* means that the owner asked for an outcome and the builder chose the means. The owner has not yet reviewed the means.
- *Pending the owner* means that hard rule 2 requires the owner's approval and the owner has not yet given it. Where work already happened, the entry says so.
- *Recorded* means that the builder judged the entry to be inside the approved plan and outside hard rule 2.
