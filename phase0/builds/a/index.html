    Your summary

    While you wait: keep gathering the items on your list, and keep your paperwork together in one place. If a date is close or you are worried about your safety, call 555-0142 rather than waiting.

    What to bring

    A general list for any first conversation with the clinic. Bring what you have. You can still contact the clinic if something is missing.

    1. The notice or letter you received, with its envelope if you have itBring every page, even the ones that look unimportant.
    2. Any other papers about the same issueLetters, forms, messages, or receipts. Bring them even if you are not sure they matter.
    3. A photo ID if you have oneIf you do not have one, you can still contact the clinic.
    4. A list of dates and events in orderShort notes are enough: what happened, and when.
    5. Names of other people or organizations involvedFor example a landlord, an employer, an agency, or a company.
    6. Your questions for the clinicWrite them down so you do not have to remember them on the day.

    Please do not send documents through this site. It cannot accept uploads, and it does not ask you to describe what happened.

    Privacy

    This site asks for as little as possible.

    What you are asked for

    • A general help topic.
    • Whether your paperwork shows a printed date, and the date itself only if you choose to enter it.
    • Which checklist items you already have.
    • A first name and one contact detail: a phone number or an email address.

    What you are never asked for

    • An account or a password.
    • Your documents, or photos of them.
    • A description of what happened.

    Where your answers are kept

    Your answers are saved in this browser, on this device, so you do not lose them if a request fails or you close the page. This is a prototype: nothing is sent over the internet.

    If you share this phone or computer with someone else, clear your answers when you finish.
