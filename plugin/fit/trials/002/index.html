What we do

Three kinds of visit cover most of what people need.

  • Checkups and cleanings

    A full look, a careful clean, and photographs of anything worth watching, explained in plain words.

  • Repairs

    Fillings, crowns, and chipped teeth. You see the price and the options before any work starts.

  • Urgent visits

    Pain, swelling, or a broken tooth. Call before ten and we will see you the same day when a chair is free.
