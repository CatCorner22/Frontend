## Summary

What the search engine's summary says about gothic styling on websites. It matters to this project because the Direction "Galactic Goth" rests on it. The evidence is weak: only the search summary was seen, and the summary says that most pages are design blogs and palette sites.

## Key concepts

- The base is near-black, with deep red ("oxblood", "blood-red") and dark purple ("plum", "royal purple"). Cold silver or antique gold appears as the only relief.
- Headings often use blackletter or other ornate lettering.
- A dark page stands out because fewer sites are dark, but it fails quickly when the text blurs into the background. The summary calls this advice its most reliable part.
- Advice repeated across pages: pair the ornate parts with plain, neutral parts so that the design does not overwhelm; use texture so that a dark page does not read as an empty void.
