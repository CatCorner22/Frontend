## Summary

What the search engine's summary says about screens made to look futuristic, as in films and games. It matters to this project because the Direction "Vector Horizon" rests on it. The evidence is weak: only the search summary was seen.

## Key concepts

- The look is a dark screen with neon colors, terminal-style lettering, and parts borrowed from a pilot's head-up display.
- Named parts of the style: grids, diagrams, technical read-outs, scanning effects, and layered see-through panels.
- Glow is common, and so are glowing outlines of shapes ("wireframes").
- One article argues that such a screen must stay usable: the future look may not get in the way of the work.
